About diSKULbre

A public slicer for the DepEd BEIS school masterlist.

Data source

This site is built on the Basic Education Information System (BEIS) school masterlist published by the Department of Education Philippines. The dataset covers all registered schools in the Philippines — public, private, and government-run colleges — as recorded in the DepEd BEIS masterlist.

Snapshot date: SY 2020 - 2021

  • DepEd — SY 2020–2021 Masterlist of Schools (PDF)
  • Kaggle — DepEd Philippine School Masterlist 2020–21

What this data does not include

  • Enrollment or student population counts
  • Teacher counts or learner-teacher ratios
  • GPS coordinates or precise school locations
  • Academic performance or NAT results
  • School facilities or infrastructure data
  • Contact information or school hours

DiSKULbre is an independent civic project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to the Department of Education Philippines in any way. Data is reproduced for public interest use.
